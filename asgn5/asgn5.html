Notes to Grader:

- Wow factor: The fog effect can be turned off and on using the buttons below.

- When fog is off, the skybox is visible as the background.

- near, far, and color controls the fog.

- Nore possible Wow factors: The scene includes bouncing spheres and a glowing orb.

- The scene also includes a textured 3D model of a spinning marble bust.

- Used four types of lights: Ambient Light (soft gray), Directional Light (sunlight and shadows), Point Light (lamp posts), and Hemisphere Light (sky/ground simulation).

- Use the WASD keys to move and click the canvas to enable pointer lock controls. Click esc button to disable pointer lock controls.

- 'Controls' drop down controls the shadows.
